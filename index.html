Official district matching

Select the city or town where you are registered to vote, then your ward (if applicable) and precinct. We use official precinct and legislative-district records to identify the contests that apply to you.

Not sure of your ward or precinct?

Enter your residential address in the Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth’s official tool, then return here and select the ward and precinct it provides.
